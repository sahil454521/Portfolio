Desi Totes

Client project, commerce. desitotes.com ↗

Cotton canvas, cut and printed after the order arrives. Nothing sits in a warehouse, so nothing can be vague.

Fabric weight, finished size, zip or no zip and the price gap between them all have to land before anyone reaches a cart. It takes cards through Razorpay, switches currency in place, and keeps a bag that survives a refresh.

AMG Turnkey Projects

Software developer, January to June 2025. amgprojectsllp.com ↗

Twenty five years old, based in Pune, and they build the factory and then fit out the inside of it. Six service lines across three states.

The brief was findability. Somebody who needs a hospital block built should reach the right service, the right past project and a way to ask, inside one pass of the page, on a site office connection.

Behind the site I built the civil accounting modules and REST APIs their operations team runs projects on, then found where the app was slow and cut its load time by 50 to 80 percent.

Both ends, or it is not finished.

A shop that renders beautifully and loses a cart is not a shop. I build the surface and the thing underneath it, and I stay on afterwards, which is the part that decides whether either was any good. A model that scores well and never leaves the notebook is the same mistake.

  1. Interface

    React, Next.js and Vite, Tailwind for the system, GSAP and Three.js where motion has to carry meaning. Real markup, keyboard order, reduced motion honoured.

  2. Service

    Node and Express, FastAPI and Flask, over MongoDB, Firestore or Convex. Catalogue, cart, orders and auth, and Razorpay with the states that go wrong handled.

  3. Models

    PyTorch, TensorFlow and Hugging Face for the deep work, scikit-learn when a small model is the right answer. Evaluation that never peeks at the test set.

  4. Running

    Vercel and Render, CI on every push, image budgets and lazy media. Then the part nobody photographs: fixing it in production.
