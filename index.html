Client 02 · Commerce

Desi Totes

Cotton canvas, cut and printed after the order arrives. Nothing sits in a warehouse, so nothing can be vague.

Fabric weight, finished size, zip or no zip and the price gap between them all have to land before anyone reaches a cart. It takes cards through Razorpay, switches currency in place, and keeps a bag that survives a refresh.

The work behind it

Both ends, or it is not finished.

A shop that renders beautifully and loses a cart is not a shop. I build the surface and the thing underneath it, and I stay on afterwards, which is the part that decides whether either was any good.

  1. Interface

    React and Vite, Tailwind for the system, GSAP where scroll has to carry meaning. Real markup, keyboard order, reduced motion honoured.

  2. Service

    Node and Express over MongoDB. Catalogue, cart, orders and the admin side of them. Razorpay for cards, with the states that go wrong handled.

  3. Running

    A content security policy, image budgets, lazy media, and a deploy that does not need me awake. Then the part nobody photographs: fixing it in production.
