# Signal: design spec (approved concept, 2026-10-03)

Dark portfolio for Sahil Pathak, AI/ML and full-stack engineer. Replaces the light gallery version.

## Idea
One amber signal. A WebGL particle field reacts to the cursor; on scroll it separates into four strands
(Interface, Service, Models, Running), braids them and fuses them into one beam: "Both ends, or it is
not finished." The same fusion is the PPEMDD paper (four signals, one gated answer).

## Chapters
1. Hero: centred headline over the particle field, two CTAs.
2. Braid: pinned, scrubbed WebGL; four strand labels, then the beam and the statement.
3. Desi Totes, AMG: pinned split; CSS 3D laptop lid opens on scroll to the real screenshot; live ping to the real URL; counters.
4. Manifesto: words scrub from dim to bright, screenshots inline in the sentence.
5. PPEMDD: toggles, strands reweight, amber signals flow in and out; metrics count up.
6. Stack bento (gapless 4x3): draggable skill constellation linked only to projects that really use each skill, live pings of all six sites, real metrics, roles wanted, resume and GitHub.
7. Also live: horizontal accordion of three side projects.
8. Resume: stacking cards (experience, education, recognition).
9. Marquee of the stack, then a massive contact CTA.
Global: glass pill nav, Cmd/Ctrl+K command palette (cmdk), Lenis on the GSAP ticker, magnetic buttons, grain.

## System
Void #08090B, panels #0F1114 / #16191E, text #EDEFF2, dim #8A919B, one accent amber #FFB547.
Cabinet Grotesk (display), Satoshi (body), Geist Mono (data only).
Motion follows prefers-reduced-motion by default, with a visible Motion toggle and ?motion override.

## Truth rules
Only real copy, numbers and links from the existing portfolio. No invented skill-to-project links.
